# Scoring rubric

Use this to score yourself on the Section F questions of any session. Every session has four question types:

- **A Conceptual**: explain why, predict what happens, identify a misconception, compare two systems.
- **B Numerical**: apply equations, use correct units, show calculations, check the answer is physically reasonable.
- **C Graph and simulation interpretation**: predict the direction of change, explain the curve's shape, find a maximum or minimum, interpret units.
- **D Design and optimisation challenge**: meet a set of constraints, compare alternatives, justify assumptions, explain trade-offs.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Conceptual understanding | 25% |
| Mathematical accuracy and units | 25% |
| Graph and simulation interpretation | 20% |
| Practical application and design decisions | 20% |
| Explanation, assumptions and validation | 10% |

Tip for beginners: put more effort on visual reasoning than on algebra at first. As you move up the sessions, give more weight to your assumptions, checks against a trusted result, and uncertainty.
